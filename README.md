One line. One autocorrect fail.

> "I have a job intervention at 10am, wish me luck."

The phone meant "job interview". The phone wrote "job intervention". The sender hit send anyway.

Entry for the [Funniest Autocorrect Fail](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-funniest-autocorrect-fail-ddf82154) bounty on make.fun — `main.ts` writes the line exactly to `/output/entry.txt`, deterministically, with no network, clock, or randomness.
